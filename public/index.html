How to play

Goal: first to 200 points.

On your turn, tap HIT to draw or STAY to bank your round points.

Bust: draw a number you already have and you lose all round points (unless you have a Second Chance).

Flip 7: draw 7 different numbers in a round and you score +15 bonus and end the round.

Action cards: Freeze locks a player out. Flip 3 forces a player to draw 3 more. Second Chance saves you from one bust.

Modifiers: +2/+4/+6/+8/+10 add to your score. ×2 doubles your number-card total.
